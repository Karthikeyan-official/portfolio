03 · AI workflow

Agent-assisted action tracker

One record for action items, with comments, mentions, evidence, and roles, plus an agent path that drafts the next step from the thread.

Resolution time about 40% faster.

The problem

Tasks were living in email, Slack, and spreadsheets.

  • No owner on a task
  • Items missed between tools
  • No evidence trail
  • Status updates by email

What shipped

A single action-point system with threaded comments, mentions, evidence, and roles. An agent drafts the next step from the thread. React, NestJS, MongoDB, and Socket.io, with JWT and RBAC in front of the data.

Resolution time about 40% faster once the thread and the owner lived in one record.
